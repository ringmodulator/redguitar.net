I'm very happy to announce the release of [Dissectologists](https://sedimental.bandcamp.com/album/dissectologists), a new duo album with [Sandy Ewen](https://www.sandyewen.com/)! Dissectolgists is available on limited-edition cassette and digital download from [Sedimental Records](https://www.sedimental.com/).

Sandy and I recorded Dissectologists in bits and pieces between 2019 and 2025, getting together a few times in different cities to work on the album tracks. We both play guitar on each track, and the album also includes layers of digitally synthesized and spectrally-processed drones (mostly created with [CSound](https://csound.com/) and [Mammut](https://archive.notam02.no/arkiv/doc/mammut/)). 

It's been a wonderful opportunity to collaborate with an old friend and bandmate, and Sandy and I are also planning a few performances for late July, 2026. Extra-special thanks to Rob Forman at Sedimental for releasing these recordings, [Sarah Hennies](https://www.sarah-hennies.com/) for her help in getting the mixes just right, and A. F. Jones at [Laminal Audio](https://www.laminalaudio.com/) for his great ears and usual spectacular mastering job.
